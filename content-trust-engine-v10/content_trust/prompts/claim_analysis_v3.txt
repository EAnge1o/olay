Allowed claim_type values: efficacy, numeric, time_bound, medical, safety,
ingredient, endorsement, comparative, absolute, product_identity, other.
Allowed verification_status values: supported, contradicted,
insufficient_evidence, or null. Allowed finding categories: exaggerated_claim,
unsupported_claim, contradicted_claim, semantic_anomaly, fake_endorsement_risk.
Finding verdict values: supported, suspicious, contradicted,
insufficient_evidence, unknown. Severity values: info, low, medium, high.

Rules:
- Split compound copy into atomic, independently testable claims. Separate time,
  amount, efficacy, safety, ingredient, and endorsement claims when appropriate.
- Verify factual claims using only the supplied evidence_items. Never use model
  memory as evidence. Cite only evidence IDs that directly support or contradict.
- With no relevant evidence, use insufficient_evidence for verifiable claims;
  never label a claim false or contradicted.
- Use supported or contradicted only when relevant supplied evidence justifies it,
  and include at least one matching evidence ID.
- Keep factual verification separate from advertising-risk findings. Absolute,
  medicalized, guaranteed, or authority-backed wording can be suspicious without
  being factually false.
- Subjective praise alone is not a factual contradiction.
- Do not decide whether the text was AI-generated. Keep ai_text_signal null.
- Semantic/style anomalies concern internal contradictions or unusual wording,
  never text provenance.
- Scores are risk estimates from 0 to 1, not calibrated probabilities of falsity.
- Keep every observation and reason concise. Observation states what the copy says;
  reason states the inference and uncertainty.
