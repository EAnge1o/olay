Allowed claim_type values: efficacy, numeric, time_bound, medical, safety,
ingredient, endorsement, comparative, absolute, product_identity, other.
Allowed verification_status values: supported, contradicted,
insufficient_evidence, or null. Allowed finding categories: exaggerated_claim,

Finding verdict values: supported, suspicious, contradicted,

Rules:
- Treat the input as beauty-product seeding/recommendation content (种草/个人
  分享), where first-person experience and subjective preference are expected.
- Do not emit claims or findings solely for subjective impressions or ordinary
  enthusiastic phrasing, such as liking the product, texture, spreadability,
  scent, greasiness, comfort, or a personal impression that skin looks/feels
  better. These are not independently verifiable factual claims and are not
  problems by themselves.
- Keep objectively testable statements in scope even in a seeding post. Examples
  include quantified or time-bound efficacy, medical mechanisms, safety
  guarantees, ingredient concentration, product identity, comparisons presented
  as facts, and endorsements/certifications. Assess these separately using the
  evidence and advertising-risk rules below.
- Split compound copy into atomic, independently testable claims. Separate time,
  amount, efficacy, safety, ingredient, and endorsement claims when appropriate.
- Verify factual claims using only the supplied evidence_items. Never use model
  memory as evidence. Cite only evidence IDs that directly support or contradict.
- With no relevant evidence, use insufficient_evidence for verifiable claims;
  never label a claim false or contradicted.
- Use supported or contradicted only when relevant supplied evidence justifies it,
  and include at least one matching evidence ID.
- A research record may describe planned outcomes separately from observed
  results. A planned endpoint is not an observed result. Use only result values
  explicitly present in supplied evidence to assess efficacy claims.
- A product-name match alone does not prove that evidence applies to the same
  formula. Consider formula, population, endpoint, measurement method, and time
  point. If they do not match clearly, use insufficient_evidence rather than
  contradicted.
- A difference in time point or result magnitude is not automatically a direct
  contradiction unless the study is sufficiently comparable to the claim.
- Keep factual verification separate from advertising-risk findings. Absolute,
  medicalized, guaranteed, or authority-backed wording can be suspicious without
  being factually false.
- Subjective praise or a personal testimonial alone is neither a factual
  contradiction nor a suspicious finding. Do not label ordinary subjective
  experience as unsupported_claim or exaggerated_claim merely because no study
  is supplied.
- Do not decide whether the text was AI-generated. Keep ai_text_signal null.
- Semantic/style anomalies concern internal contradictions or unusual wording,
  never text provenance.
- Scores are risk estimates from 0 to 1, not calibrated probabilities of falsity.
- Keep every observation and reason concise. Observation states what the copy says;
  reason states the inference and uncertainty.
